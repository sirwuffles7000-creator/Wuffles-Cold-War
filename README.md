Welcome to a true semi-anarchy experience powered by HBM’s Nuclear Tech Mod. Build massive factories, enrich uranium, launch nukes, and fight over territory. Griefing, raiding, and PvP are fully allowed

Rules
Minimal rules: no hacking, no x-ray, no intentional lag machines. Everything else is fair game.
Essential WarMC Commands
Command	Description
/warmcclaims team create <name>	Create a team (short unique tag)
/warmcclaims team invite <player>	Invite a player to your team
/warmcclaims team accept	Accept a pending team invite
/warmcclaims team kick <player>	Kick a teammate (owner only)
/warmcclaims team leave	Leave your current team
/warmcclaims team list	List your team members
/warmcclaims status	Shows claim info at your current position
 
 
How claims work (quick start):
Create or join a team.
Place a Claim Flag in the overworld (one claim per team).
Right-click the flag to open the upkeep / upgrade menu.
Keep upkeep items in the flag so protection stays active.
5. Provide Power into Claim Flag with HBM
